# Substack Content Plan — Bloom / AI Investing

## Writing Process (Human-in-the-Loop)

Inspired by Eric's [practical guide to writing with AI](https://blog.promptpm.ai/p/a-practical-guide-writing-with-ai):

### Stage 1: Ideation & Selection
- Present article ideas with SEO angle and target audience
- Eric picks which to develop first
- Research keywords for selected topic

### Stage 2: Outline & Structure  
- Generate 2-3 outline variants per article
- ASCII wireframe of article layout
- Eric picks best structure, suggests changes

### Stage 3: Visual Planning
- Excalidraw diagrams for concepts, flows, architecture
- Nano Banana Pro for hero images, illustrations
- Screenshots from Bloom app, code, terminal
- Web images where relevant (with attribution)

### Stage 4: Drafting
- Write full draft with visuals embedded
- Eric reviews, edits, adjusts tone/stance
- Iterate until ready

### Stage 5: Polish & Publish
- SEO checklist (title, subtitle, keywords, meta)
- Final review
- Publish to Substack + promote via Typefully
