- **What makes a maverick**: Framework through the lens of information asymmetry — the market is pricing in the consensus narrative, but the actual narrative is different
- **Historical case studies**: Deep dives into Tesla, Netflix, Palantir — what the consensus said, what was actually happening, when the divergence was detectable
- **AI as your edge in the information game**: AI can process the full information surface area — every earnings call, every insider trade, every sentiment shift — and find where the gap is
- **Building a maverick portfolio**: How to size positions, manage risk, and avoid blowing up when your mavericks turn out to be duds
- **Bloom's tools**: Automated narrative divergence detection, contrarian conviction scoring
- **Close**: The market rewards people who are right about things that are hard to be right about. AI makes it less hard.
