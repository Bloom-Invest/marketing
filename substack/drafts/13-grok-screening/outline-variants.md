# Article #13 — Outline Variants

## Variant A: "The Twitter Whisperer" (Tutorial-First)

1. **Hook**: Open with a specific stock that blew up on Twitter days before it moved — and you caught it via Grok
2. **Why Grok for stocks**: Real-time X/Twitter integration, social sentiment as a leading indicator
3. **The prompts that actually work**: Specific copy-paste prompts, organized by use case
4. **Filtering garbage from signal**: 90% of stock Twitter is trash — here's how to not get wrecked
5. **Pairing Grok with Bloom**: Social hype → fundamental validation pipeline
6. **Real examples**: 3-4 stocks that showed social momentum before price movement
7. **Honest limitations**: Grok hallucinates numbers, doesn't replace real analysis

*Pros*: Very practical, high utility, easy to follow
*Cons*: Could read too much like a how-to guide, less personality

6. **The pipeline: Grok finds it, Bloom validates it**: Social signal → fundamental check workflow
7. **Receipts**: Real examples of stocks that showed up on social before they moved (with dates/prices)
8. **Where Grok will absolutely screw you**: Hallucinated financials, false confidence, the dangers
9. **Close**: This isn't a strategy. It's one tool in the toolbox.

*Pros*: Best narrative arc, matches Eric's voice perfectly, honest and opinionated
*Cons*: Longer, needs good examples to land
