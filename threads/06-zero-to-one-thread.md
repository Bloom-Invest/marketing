### Post 2
Palantir's Q4 revenue: $1.41B. Up from $884M in Q1. Each quarter steps up $150-230M.

They were losing over a billion a year in 2020. Now: 36% net margin, $1.63B TTM net income, $7.18B cash, essentially no debt.

Hard to argue with that part.

### Post 3
Every "how to research stocks" guide says read the 10-K. The 10-K is 200+ pages of SEC legalese written by lawyers for lawyers.

Skip it. Read the earnings call transcript. 15 minutes. CEO goes off-script, analysts ask hostile questions, and silence on a topic is data.

### Post 4

But P/E screenshots on Twitter miss the point. Revenue growth: 63% YoY. Operating income up 248%. PEG ratio: ~2.8.

Datadog trades at 313x. Snowflake isn't even profitable.
