### Post 2
I ran a quick DCF on Shopify at $105.

Bull case: ~$118. Bear case (growth slows to 15%): ~$61. Multiple compression alone: ~$72 even with solid execution.

Upside: 12%. Downside: 30-42%. Asymmetric — and not in my favor.

### Post 3
Here's what nobody tells you about Wall Street models: they're all wrong. Every single one.

The value isn't the final number. It's making your assumptions explicit. Growth rate: 20% or 25%? Each assumption is a lever. Pull them and see what breaks.

### Post 4
The real trap is false precision. A model that says "$93.47 per share" is lying to you.

The honest answer is a range. And the width of that range IS the point. Bull: $120. Bear: $70. That gap tells you how much uncertainty exists.
