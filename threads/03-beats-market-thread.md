# Thread: AI Stock Research in 4 Minutes
## Source: /Users/testuser/marketing/substack/drafts/03-beats-market/draft.md

### Post 1 (Hook)
I used to spend 3 days researching a single stock. Last Tuesday a friend texted me about @HIMS. I typed one sentence into an AI agent and had a full bull/bear thesis in 4 minutes. Zero spreadsheets.

### Post 2
The first page of Google for "how to research a stock" is all Fidelity and NerdWallet fluff. "Check the P/E ratio." "Read the 10-K." As if anyone is wading through 200 pages of SEC filings on a Tuesday night.

### Post 3
Stock research is actually 6 jobs: financials, valuation, growth, competitive position, insider activity, catalysts. The old way? 2-3 hours minimum per stock. A proper model with scenarios: a full day. I have 30+ stocks on my watchlist. That math doesn't work.

### Post 4
Here's what the AI caught on @HIMS in 4 minutes: 69% YoY revenue growth, 82% gross margins (software-level on a health company), net income just turned positive, 2M+ recurring subscribers, multiple insiders buying. ~30x forward earnings for that growth rate isn't expensive.

### Post 5
But here's the honest part — AI can't read a room. Is the CEO competent or just good at conference calls? Is a company getting creative with accounting? It trusts bad data confidently. It's the best research analyst you've never been able to afford. But it's junior. You're still the PM.

### Post 6 (Closer)
The advantage in 2025 isn't data access — everyone has data. It's speed of analysis. Research 50 stocks in the time it takes someone else to research 5. That's just math.
