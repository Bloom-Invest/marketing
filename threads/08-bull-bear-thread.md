# Thread: Your Portfolio Has a Blind Spot
## Source: /Users/testuser/marketing/substack/drafts/08-bull-bear/draft.md

### Post 1 (Hook)
I scrolled through my 23-stock watchlist. Detailed bull case on every single one. Number of documented bear cases: zero.

That's not a portfolio. That's a prayer.

### Post 2
89% of sell-side ratings are Buy or Hold. Fewer than 6% are Sell.

Banks don't win investment banking mandates by telling a CEO their stock is overvalued. The entire research machine is structurally built to make you bullish.

### Post 3
I did this with Peloton in 2021. Revenue up 100%+! Recurring subs! Future of fitness!

Never once asked: what happens when gyms reopen?

$PTON went from $160 to $3. Thirty thousand dollars in lessons.

### Post 4
The fix: make AI argue against you. Not "analyze HIMS for me" — that gets a diplomatic nothing-burger.

Try: "I'm long at $55. Convince me I'm wrong. Be specific. Use numbers. Pretend you're a short seller who's done their homework."

Completely different output.

### Post 5
Ran this on $HIMS. Bull case: $85. Bear case: $28. 50% upside vs 50% downside — symmetrical risk/reward.

Without the bear case, I would've bought on excitement. With it, I realized I needed a better entry point.

### Post 6
The bear case changes behavior in ways that compound over years:

Better entries. Prepared drawdowns. Smarter position sizing.

The bull case gets you interested. The bear case keeps you from blowing up.

Full breakdown: https://investwithbloom.com
