# Thread: Stop Watching EPS
## Source: /Users/testuser/marketing/substack/drafts/11-key-drivers/draft.md

### Post 1 (Hook)
Chipotle missed on EPS by $0.27. Stock went UP 4%.

Same-store sales: +6%. Transaction growth: positive 4th straight quarter. New openings: on track for 300+.

EPS didn't matter. Not even a little.

### Post 2
Every stock has 1-2 metrics that explain 80% of its price move. It's almost never EPS.

$AMZN: AWS growth rate. $NFLX: paid subscriber net adds. $CRWD: net new ARR. $META in 2023: operating expenses.

The rest is noise.

### Post 3
$META went from $90 to $570. The single best predictor? OpEx.

Zuckerberg cut 21,000 employees. Operating margin went from 20% to 35% in three quarters. One number. 6x return.

### Post 4
EPS is the output of dozens of business decisions, accounting choices, tax rates, and buybacks all mashed into one number.

Buybacks alone inflate EPS 2-3% per year without the business improving at all. A company can "beat" earnings by repurchasing stock while revenue flatlines.

### Post 5
Finding key drivers the old way means reading 4-6 quarters of earnings transcripts per stock. That's 4-5 hours of reading. Across a 15-stock portfolio? Sixty hours.

That's why most investors default to EPS. Not because it's right — because it's easy.

### Post 6
One question cuts through all of it: "What does the market care about for THIS specific stock?"

Pick a stock you own. Read the last two earnings calls. What does the CEO open with? What do analysts keep asking about? What metric actually moved the stock when it surprised?

That's your key driver. Stop watching everything else: https://investwithbloom.com
