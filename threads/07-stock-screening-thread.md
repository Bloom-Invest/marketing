### Post 1 (Hook)
"Find me companies where revenue is accelerating quarter over quarter, insiders are buying, and it's still cheap relative to growth."

Any analyst understands that instantly. Try typing it into Finviz. You can't.

### Post 2
I ran that screen last month. 11 names came back. Most were boring small industrials I'd never heard of.

One was HIMS. Revenue growth had gone from 47% to 69% over three quarters. Multiple insiders buying. ~30x forward earnings.

Six weeks later, HIMS ran 40%.

### Post 3
Finviz, Yahoo Finance, TradingView — fundamentally the same product they were in 2010. Set numerical thresholds, database returns matching rows.

Fine if your thesis is a set of checkboxes. Most good theses aren't.

### Post 4
A company growing revenue at 15% isn't exciting. A company that grew 8%, then 12%, then 15%, then 22% over four quarters — that's a business inflecting.

The trajectory matters more than any single number. No checkbox screener filters by trajectory.

### Post 5
But here's where I have to be honest: for every HIMS, there were probably 3 names from the same screen that flatlined.

I ran 14 screens last month. Hit rate: maybe 1 in 5 turned into an actual investment idea. Screening is a funnel, not a stock picker.

### Post 6 (Closer)
Metal detector on the beach. It beeps. You dig. Sometimes a ring, sometimes a bottle cap. The detector doesn't know the difference. You do.

AI makes screening faster and more expressive. But the thinking starts at the results — not stops.
